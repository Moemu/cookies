# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the cookies community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, discriminatory language, trolling, personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, or other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. Reports may be made privately as described in [SUPPORT.md](./SUPPORT.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove, reject, or moderate contributions and may apply warnings, temporary restrictions, or permanent bans proportionate to the behavior.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.
