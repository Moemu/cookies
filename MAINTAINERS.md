## Maintainer expectations

Maintainers follow [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md), uphold the [Code of Conduct](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), protect contributor and customer data, review changes within their expertise, and avoid merging changes with unresolved security or compatibility concerns.

Maintainer status does not grant authority over third-party accounts, customer data, or external services.
